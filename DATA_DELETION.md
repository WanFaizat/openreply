# Data Deletion Instructions — WanFaizat OpenReply

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

If you interacted with Instagram account **@faims_** and would like to request deletion of personal information processed by **WanFaizat OpenReply**, you may submit a request to the operator.

## How to request deletion

1. Contact the operator using the contact information on https://www.wanfaizat.com/ or initiate a request through https://github.com/WanFaizat/openreply/issues.
2. If you use a public GitHub issue, state only that you wish to request data deletion. **Do not publish** your Instagram username, personal data, access tokens, private conversations or identity documents in a public issue.
3. Arrange a private communication channel with the operator and provide only the information reasonably needed to identify the interaction or account.
4. The operator may verify that you control the relevant account before locating and deleting eligible data.

Requests will be handled as reasonably practicable, subject to legal, security and platform retention requirements.

## Disconnecting the Instagram account

The authorized administrator may disconnect the connected Instagram account in the OpenReply settings to stop related automated actions. To request deletion of historical logs or other retained account information, follow the procedure above.
