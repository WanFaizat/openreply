# Terms of Service — WanFaizat OpenReply

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

WanFaizat OpenReply is a self-hosted tool used to manage permitted Instagram comment-to-message and messaging automations for the Instagram account **@faims_**.

## Authorized use

Only authorized account owners and administrators may configure or operate this service. Campaigns, keywords and messages must comply with Meta Platform Terms, Instagram rules and applicable laws, including messaging and anti-spam restrictions.

## Information and guides

Automated messages may provide links to educational information, including guides about saving gold. They do not guarantee financial outcomes. Users should independently assess whether any financial product suits their needs.

## Availability and third-party services

The service depends on Instagram/Meta APIs, hosting, databases and background workers. Availability and message delivery are not guaranteed, and individual messages may be delayed, blocked or limited under Meta's policies.
