# Privacy Policy — WanFaizat OpenReply

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **WanFaizat OpenReply**, a self-hosted Instagram automation service operated for **@faims_**, processes information when people interact with the Instagram account or when an authorized administrator uses the OpenReply dashboard.

## Information processed

The service may process:
- Instagram account identifiers, usernames, profile identifiers, post and comment identifiers, and comment text relevant to a configured automation campaign.
- Messages and reply events received through authorized Instagram/Meta APIs, including messages that contain campaign keywords and button interactions.
- Campaign settings, keywords, outgoing messages, delivery status, timestamps, logs, and technical error records.
- The administrator's login email and technical session information.
- Encrypted access tokens and related account authorization data needed to connect the authorized Instagram account.

## Why the information is used

Information is processed to recognize comments or messages containing campaign keywords, send requested guides or automatic replies, handle follow-up interactions, prevent duplicate messages, maintain account connections, detect misuse, and troubleshoot failures.

## Meta and third-party services

The service uses Meta's official Instagram APIs. Information is transmitted to Meta as necessary to read permitted interactions and send authorized replies. The self-hosted service also relies on infrastructure such as web hosting, databases and message queues (including GitHub Codespaces in its development environment). These service providers may process the technical information necessary to operate the service. The operator does not sell Instagram interaction data.

## Storage and retention

Campaign activity, interaction records and technical logs may be retained for service operation and troubleshooting. Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes and applicable legal or platform requirements. Access is limited to the operator and authorized personnel. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and data deletion

If you wish to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information processed by this service, contact the operator using the [project contact page](https://github.com/WanFaizat/openreply/issues) or the contact information available on [WanFaizat's website](https://www.wanfaizat.com/). When opening a public issue, do **not** include personal identifiers, private messages, or other sensitive data; request a private channel for those details. Requests may require verification of account ownership. See the [Data Deletion Instructions](./DATA_DELETION.md).

An authorized administrator can disconnect the Instagram account through OpenReply settings to stop further automated processing associated with that connection.

## Updates

This notice may be updated to reflect changes in the service and its data practices. The date above will be updated for material revisions.
